# Nimbus Labs Security Awareness and Workplace Policies (v1.5)

## Security training
Every new employee completes the mandatory security induction within their first 5 working days. The induction takes about 90 minutes and includes modules on phishing, password managers, data classification, and how to report incidents. Access to production systems is not granted until the induction is passed with a score of at least 80 percent. All employees repeat a shorter refresher each September, and engineers also take a secure coding course every year in February.

## Reporting suspicious messages
Employees report suspected phishing using the Hook It button in their email client. The button forwards the message to the security team and removes it from the mailbox. Reports receive an automated reply within a minute, and a human analyst follows up within 4 business hours for confirmed threats. Employees are never punished for reporting a message that turns out to be harmless, and the company celebrates good reports with a monthly shout-out.

## Phishing simulations
The security team sends simulated phishing emails every month. The target is a click rate below 4 percent and a report rate above 60 percent. Employees who click are shown a short, friendly explanation immediately rather than being blamed. Anyone who clicks on three simulations in a row is invited to a one-to-one coaching session of 30 minutes.

## Company devices
Every laptop has full disk encryption enabled, an endpoint detection agent, and an automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity. Employees may not disable these controls. Software must come from the approved catalogue, and requests for new software go through a ticket that is answered within 3 business days. Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 1 hour so the device can be locked and wiped remotely.

## Personal devices
Bring-your-own-device is allowed only for email and chat through a managed mobile application. Personal devices may never be used to access Restricted data. A personal phone that is used for work must have a passcode of at least 6 digits and must be running a supported version of its operating system. When the employee leaves, only the work container is wiped, not the whole phone.

## Security champions
Every engineering and product team nominates one security champion. Champions spend about 10 percent of their time on security activities, such as reviewing designs and triaging scanner findings. They meet every second Tuesday for a 45 minute guild session, and they receive additional training each quarter. Champions are recognised in performance reviews, and the program currently has a goal of covering every team by the end of the financial year.

## Security office hours
The security team holds open office hours every Thursday at 15:00 IST in the Mulberry meeting room and on video call. Anyone can bring a question about architecture, vendor risk, or personal account safety. A short design review can be booked in advance for up to 60 minutes, and teams are encouraged to book one before launching any new feature that handles personal data.

## Travel
Employees traveling to countries on the high-risk list receive a clean loaner laptop and a temporary phone. Personal and work data must not be carried on these devices, and they are reimaged on return. Employees should avoid connecting to hotel or airport networks without the VPN, and must report any border inspection of a device to the security team the same day.

## Working in public places
Employees should not discuss Confidential topics in cafes, trains, or co-working spaces. Privacy screens are available on request and are recommended for anyone who works regularly in public. Video calls should use a neutral or blurred background, and screens must be locked when stepping away even for a moment.

## Vendor risk
Before signing with a new vendor that will handle Confidential or Restricted data, the procurement team sends a security questionnaire of 60 questions and reviews the vendor's independent audit report. Vendors are rated low, medium, or high risk. High risk vendors are reassessed every 6 months, while low risk vendors are reassessed every 24 months. A vendor that suffers a breach must notify Nimbus Labs within 24 hours.

## Consequences and support
Deliberate violations of these policies may lead to disciplinary action, but honest mistakes are treated as learning opportunities. The security team prefers early reporting over perfect behaviour. Anyone who is unsure about a policy should ask in the security channel or during office hours, and managers are expected to support their teams in doing so.
